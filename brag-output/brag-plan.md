# Brag Plan: MatchDesk

## What is this app?
MatchDesk turns a football match event stream into overlay-ready insight where every sentence cites the events behind it and a Verifier blocks anything it cannot prove.

## The angle
Apple-keynote restraint applied to a trust problem: big quiet type, one idea per scene, and the proof as the hero (a real sentence, its evidence ids, and the measured block rate). No jokes.

## Hook (0-3.5 s)
"Every number on screen." then "had to prove itself."

## Key moments
- A real goal sentence from the public recorded run, typed in, with its source badge ("Written by an AI model, checked by the Verifier") and the cited event ids appearing as chips.
- The measured Verifier result: 3,424 of 3,426 corrupted claims blocked, 0 clean claims wrongly rejected.
- Two languages: English and Bahasa Indonesia.

## Outro / punchline
"MatchDesk. Nothing on screen without proof."

## User flow worth showing
Match event arrives -> sentence is written and checked -> card shows the evidence ids.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film, keynote pacing
- Interpretation: slow-in, long holds, large type, no aggression, one idea per scene.

## Format: landscape - 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #000000 (video), card surface #171b21 (app dark theme)
- Accent: #6aa5ff (app dark accent)
- Text: #e8ebef
- Fonts: system sans (SF Pro / Helvetica Neue)
- Strongest visual element: the key-moment card with the evidence chips

## Share copy (draft)
